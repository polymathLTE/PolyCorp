You are redesigning and rebuilding the entire Polymath Corporation website:

https://www.polymathcorp.works/

Treat this as a full brand, UX, content, information architecture, visual design and implementation revamp, not a cosmetic refresh.

Before changing anything, inspect the existing website, all accessible routes, existing components, content, project data, assets, typography, spacing, responsive behaviour, metadata, and interactions. Understand what is currently there, then replace weak or stale material with a coherent new system.

Use the existing site as source material, not as the design template.

Also use the attached Polymath Corporation logo as the primary visual identity reference.

The goal is to make Polymath feel like a serious, technically sophisticated, builder-led engineering studio. It should feel credible to technical recruiters, founders, potential clients, collaborators and engineering teams.

==================================================

Good:

Good:

Weak:
"10x business growth."

Weak:
"99.9% uptime."

Weak:
"50M+ records processed."

unless those values are actually documented, measurable and attributable to the relevant project.

Whenever a performance number is used, provide enough context to understand:

- what was measured
- what dataset/system was measured
- timeframe
- evaluation method
- relevant conditions

Prefer precise and defensible evidence over impressive-sounding claims.
